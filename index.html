About Me

Hi, I'm Priyanshi Malik, a Senior at Thomas A. Edison Career and Technical Education High School in Queens, New York. I am enrolled in the Web and App Development program, certified in HTML and CSS, and I like building things that work well and look good. I'm headed toward a career in STEM as a Biomedical Engineer.
