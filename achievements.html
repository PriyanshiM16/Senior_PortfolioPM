ACHIEVEMENTS🏆

Welcome to my Achievement portfolio! This showcases all the achievements that I have made during my time at Thomas A. Edison CTE High School. This shows the times when I have taken on leadership roles, taking myself or my team to victory.



Infy App Challange (Infosys x PENCIL)

The Infy App Challenge, run by Infosys, is a student competition for building app ideas that solve real problems — in education, health, or everyday life. Our team reached the finalist stage, which meant our concept stood out from a large field for its creativity, practicality, and clear thinking. That selection sent us to the challenge's final round, hosted by Infosys.

Yotuh and AI Summit (IOY)

On August 12, 2026, I represented Thomas A. Edison CTE High School at the Youth and AI Summit at the United Nations Headquarters, where over 600 young leaders gathered to shape the future of artificial intelligence. During my remarks, I asked whether society can embrace AI's potential in fields like healthcare and diplomacy without sacrificing personal privacy. I argued that the threat to our searches, voices, and locations comes not from the technology itself, but from opaque corporate policies and weak regulations that treat our data like a free resource. Ultimately, I called for strict data boundaries and real transparency, contributing to the 11-article Youth-led Declaration on AI Policy and Governance presented to guide global frameworks like the UN’s Global Digital Compact.
